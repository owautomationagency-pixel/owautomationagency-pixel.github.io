How the founding offer works

  • The free month starts on the day your assistant goes live on your website, not the day you sign up.
  • Before it goes live, we finish three things for you: hosting, email confirmations to your customers and connecting your calendar. We agree a start date with you and tell you exactly what’s ready before you start.
  • If you continue after the free month, the £29 setup fee and your first £49 month are charged together at the start of your first paid month. The 12 months start then.
  • After the 12 months, the price stays at £49 a month unless you agree to a change.
  • If the three founding places have been taken, we’ll tell you in writing what continuing would cost before your pilot starts.

How to subscribe

Email us to request a pilot. We confirm your plan, start date and terms by email. If you decide to continue after the free month, we send you a secure Stripe payment link, and your subscription starts when the payment succeeds.

Plans after the pilot

We plan to offer four monthly plans, from £29 to £129 a month plus a one-off setup fee. They aren’t on sale yet. We’ll publish what each one includes once those features are working, and we won’t sell a plan until everything it includes works.

Billing

All prices are in pounds sterling (GBP). We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. Payments are taken by card through Stripe, and we never see your full card number. After your first payment, only the monthly price renews, charged in advance each month. We never charge automatically for extra usage; any extra work is quoted first and done only with your agreement.

You can cancel at any time and keep the service until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Read our refund and cancellation policy and terms.
