Questions

What to expect.

Who is this for?

Independent car detailers and valeters in the UK who take enquiries online and miss some while they’re busy on a job. It is a service for businesses, not consumers.

What happens when the assistant can’t answer?

It only uses the business information you approve. Anything outside that is passed to you as an enquiry with the customer’s contact details, rather than guessed.

Is it ready today?

It’s a new product, and we’re looking for the first pilot businesses now. The first pilots start with answers and enquiries. The assistant goes live on a pilot business’s website once hosting and email alerts are ready, and we agree a start date with you. Connecting your calendar comes later. We’ll tell you exactly what’s ready before you start.

How much of my time does it take?

A short call, then checking the services, prices and hours we’ve written up for you. During the pilot we check in once a week.

What if it gets something wrong?

It only uses the information you approve. If something needs changing, tell us and we’ll update it. Anything it isn’t sure about comes to you as an enquiry.

What if I don’t like it?

You don’t need to do anything. It stops at the end of the free month unless you choose to subscribe, and there’s no card to cancel. If you’d like it to stop sooner, just tell us.

Who owns my customers’ details?

You do. We use them only to run the service for you and never contact your customers for anything else. See our privacy notice.

How do billing and cancellation work?

The pilot is free with no card needed. If you continue, you pay a one-off setup fee and a monthly subscription, with no long-term contract. You can cancel at any time and keep the service until the end of the month you’ve paid for. See refunds and cancellation.

Want to try it on your business?

Send us a quick email and we’ll reply within two business days.
