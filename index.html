What it does

Answers, enquiries and bookings.

Answers from your price list

Customers get consistent answers about your services, prices for each vehicle size and your opening hours, using only the information you approve.

Captures enquiries

When a customer has a question it can’t answer, or wants you to call back, it takes their name and contact details and saves the enquiry for you to follow up.

Helps customers book

Customers can choose an available time and confirm their booking, and you can see, move or cancel it. Connecting your own calendar is being finished for pilot businesses.

Questions

What to expect.

Who is this for?

Independent car detailers and valeters in the UK who take enquiries online and miss some while they’re busy on a job. It is a service for businesses, not consumers.

What happens when the assistant can’t answer?

It only uses the business information you approve. Anything outside that is passed to you as an enquiry with the customer’s contact details, rather than guessed.

Is it ready today?

It’s a new product, and we’re scheduling the first pilots now. The assistant goes live on a pilot business’s website once hosting, email confirmations and calendar connection are ready, and we agree a start date with you. We’ll tell you exactly what’s ready before you start.

Who owns my customers’ details?

You do. We use them only to run the service for you and never contact your customers for anything else. See our privacy notice.

How do billing and cancellation work?

The pilot is free with no card needed. If you continue, you pay a one-off setup fee and a monthly subscription, with no long-term contract. You can cancel at any time and keep the service until the end of the month you’ve paid for. See refunds and cancellation.
