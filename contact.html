Requesting a free pilot

Tell us your business name, where you’re based, how customers usually contact you (website, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp or phone) and roughly how many enquiries you get a week. We’ll reply to arrange a short call, then agree a start date with you.

Before your pilot starts, we’ll email you our terms to accept, with what’s ready and what your plan includes.

Continuing after the pilot

If you decide to continue after your free month, we send you a secure Stripe payment link. Your subscription starts when the payment succeeds. See pricing for what you’ll pay.

Help with your subscription

To cancel, change your plan, update your card or ask about a charge, email us from the email address you signed up with. We’ll confirm each request in writing. Our refund and cancellation policy explains what happens next.

A reply means we’ve acknowledged your request and started looking into it. Some issues take longer to fully resolve, and we’ll keep you updated.
