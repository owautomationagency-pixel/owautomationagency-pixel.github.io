1Who we are

2The service

We set up and run a website assistant for your business. It answers customer questions using the services, prices and opening hours you approve, takes customers’ details and saves enquiries for you to follow up, and, where your plan includes it, lets customers choose an available time and book. Each plan covers one business location and one website assistant. What your plan includes, its start date and any limits are confirmed in writing before you start.

The service is for businesses only. By using it, you confirm you are acting for your business and not as a consumer.

The assistant gives answers based on the information you provide. We don’t guarantee any particular number of enquiries, bookings or sales, or that the service will be uninterrupted.

3How the contract is formed

We confirm your plan, start date and these terms by email. For a free pilot, the contract starts when you reply to accept. For a paid subscription, it starts when your first payment succeeds. These terms are in English, and you can save or print this page.

4Free pilot

A free pilot lasts one month, starting on the day your assistant goes live on your website. It costs nothing and no card is needed. If you’d like it to stop sooner, just tell us. At the end of the pilot, the service continues only if you choose to subscribe. If you don’t, it stops and there is nothing to cancel.

5Fees and payment

  • Your fees are the ones shown on our pricing page and confirmed to you in writing. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added.
  • We take a first payment only after your assistant is live on your website and you’ve had the chance to check it. For pilot businesses, the one-off setup fee and the first month are charged together at the start of the first paid month.
  • After that, the monthly subscription is charged in advance each month. Payments are taken by card through our payment provider, Stripe.
  • The founding price is fixed for 12 months from your first paid month while your subscription stays active. After the 12 months, the price stays at £49 a month unless you agree to a change.
  • We never charge automatically for usage beyond your plan. Any extra work is quoted separately and done only with your agreement.

6Cancellation and refunds

There is no minimum term. You can cancel at any time, and the service continues until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Our refund and cancellation policy forms part of these terms.

7Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate business information (services, prices, opening hours and policies) and tell us promptly when it changes.
  • Check the answers and settings we prepare before the assistant goes live on your website.
  • Deal with your own customers’ bookings and enquiries, and make sure you’re allowed to add the assistant to your website.

8Your data and your customers’ data

Customer details captured by the assistant belong to you. You are the controller of that information, and we process it on your behalf, only to provide the service. We will agree written data processing terms with you before your assistant handles real customer information. Our privacy notice explains how we handle personal information.

Support is by email, and we’ll call you on request. We aim to reply within two business days, or one business day if your assistant has stopped working. A reply means we’ve acknowledged your request and started investigating, not that it is resolved.

We may update these terms. We’ll give you at least 30 days’ notice by email of any change that affects you, and if you don’t agree, you can cancel before it takes effect. This doesn’t allow us to change the founding price: that follows section 5.
