Who we are

OW Automation Agency is a trading name of Ollie Wood, a sole trader based at 64 Glebelands, Harlow, Essex, CM20 2PB, who is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact us about privacy at owautomationagency@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • When you contact us or request a pilot: your name, email address, business name and details, and what you tell us. We use this to reply and arrange the pilot (our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, or steps you ask us to take before a contract).
  • When you’re a customer: contact and business details, your account settings and the business information your assistant uses. We use this to provide the service under our contract with you.
  • Billing: payments are handled by Stripe. We receive payment status and limited card details (such as the last four digits), never your full card number. We keep billing records to meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • When you visit this website: our website host records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running (our legitimate interest). The host keeps these logs for a short period.

We need your name, email address and business details to reply to you and to provide the service; without them we can’t do either. We must keep billing records by law.

Businesses we contact about our service

We sometimes contact businesses we think could use our service. We use business contact details the business publishes, for example on its website, Google Business Profile or social media business pages, or that someone recommends to us. We keep the business name, the owner’s name where it’s published as part of the business, business contact details and short notes of our conversations.

We do this for our legitimate interest in finding customers. If there’s no pilot or subscription within 12 months, we delete these details. If you ask us not to contact you, we stop, and we keep only your business name and contact details on a do-not-contact list so we don’t contact you again.

Your customers’ information

When the assistant captures details from your customers (for example names, contact details and booking information), you are the controller of that information and we process it only on your instructions to provide the service. We never use it for our own marketing or contact your customers for anything else.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe processes payments for us. Stripe is also an independent controller for some processing, such as fraud prevention and meeting its own legal obligations; see Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • Google provides our email account, so emails you send us are stored by Google under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Our website and service hosting providers process information only on our behalf.
  • Our accountant and professional advisers, and HMRC or other authorities where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK’s International Data Transfer Agreement or adequacy regulations. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards we rely on.

We don’t sell personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to a pilot or subscription: deleted after 12 months.
  • Pilot businesses that don’t continue: deleted 12 months after the pilot ends.
  • Businesses we’ve contacted about our service: deleted after 12 months if there’s no pilot or subscription.
  • Customer account information: kept while you’re a customer and deleted within 90 days after you leave.
  • Support emails: deleted two years after the conversation ends.
  • Billing records: kept for six years for tax purposes.
  • Do-not-contact list: kept for as long as we need it to respect your request.
  • Your customers’ information: deleted within 90 days after the service ends, unless you ask us to return it first.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To use these rights, email us and we’ll reply within one month.

Your right to object

You can object at any time to us using your information on the basis of our legitimate interests. You can always tell us to stop contacting you about our service, and we’ll stop straight away.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking.
