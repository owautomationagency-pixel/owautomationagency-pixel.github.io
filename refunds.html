Refunds and cancellation.

No long-term contract, no cancellation fee. Here’s exactly how cancelling and refunds work.

Cancellation policy

  • Free pilot: there’s nothing to cancel. If you don’t choose to subscribe at the end of the month, the service simply stops.
  • Subscriptions: you can cancel at any time by emailing owautomationagency@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with. We’ll confirm the cancellation in writing within two business days.
  • Your service continues until the end of the month you’ve already paid for, and you won’t be charged again.
  • If you cancel during the founding 12-month period, the founding price ends with your subscription.

Refund policy

  • Monthly subscription: monthly fees are not refunded for part of a month after you cancel. You keep the service until the end of that month.
  • Setup fee (pilot businesses): your assistant is set up during the free month, and the setup fee is charged only if you choose to continue. If you don’t continue, there’s nothing to pay or refund.
  • Setup fee (other plans): refunded in full if you cancel before we start setting up your assistant. Once setup work has started, the setup fee is not refundable.
  • If we can’t provide the service: if we fail to provide the service you’ve paid for, we’ll refund the unused part of that month.
  • Charges made in error: if you’re charged twice or charged the wrong amount, we’ll refund the incorrect amount in full. Please tell us within 30 days of the charge.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Questions about a charge

Please email us before contacting your bank, so we can put things right quickly. See contact and support.
