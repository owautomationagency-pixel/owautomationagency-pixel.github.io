See it working

One minute with the assistant.

A screen recording of the real assistant on an example business’s website. It answers from the business’s own prices and opening hours, and passes anything else to the business instead of guessing.

What you just saw

  1. A customer asks a price. “How much is a full valet on a Range Rover?” The assistant gives that business’s large-car price, straight from its own price list.
  2. They check the opening hours. “Are you open Saturdays?” gets a plain-English answer from the hours the business set.
  3. It doesn’t guess. “Can you get dog hair out of a van?” isn’t in the price list, so the assistant offers to pass the question to the business.
  4. The customer leaves their details. They enter their name and email and confirm the email with a 6-digit code. (In the demo the code appears in a demo mailbox; nothing is emailed.)
  5. Their question is already filled in. They can add to it, then send. They get a reference.
  6. The enquiry lands in the business’s portal. With the customer’s name, email and question, ready to reply to.
